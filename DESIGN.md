# Design System: Umunara Ministry Website Redesign

**Stitch project:** `2400844321307753052`

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Umunara is a warm, editorial ministry presence: reverent without feeling formal, and contemporary without becoming corporate. The pages use generous open space, calm blue-tinted surfaces, large rounded content shells, and confident typography to make prayer, Scripture, and global connection feel immediately welcoming.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

- **Ministry Cobalt** `#1D52B8` — primary calls to action, active controls, and key brand moments.
- **Midnight Slate** `#101E33` — display text and high-contrast panels.
- **Canvas White** `#FAFBFD` — the soft, almost-white page ground.
- **Sky Surface** `#EBF3FA` — welcoming feature and prayer surfaces.
- **Mint Surface** `#EEF7F2` — community and impact content.
- **Lavender Surface** `#F4F2FA` — reflective Scripture and devotional content.
- **Slate Ash** `#64748B` — supporting copy and quiet metadata.
- **Subtle Border** `#E2E8F0` — fine delineation between otherwise light surfaces.
- **Living Green** `#75B259` — live-broadcast and active-status indicators.

## 3. Typography Rules

Use **Space Grotesk** for display headings and labels; its tight tracking gives headings a clear, contemporary editorial voice. Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy, forms, and metadata. Large headings are bold, compactly leading, and slightly negatively tracked. Labels are small, uppercase, and generously tracked.

## 4. Component Stylings

- **Buttons:** Cobalt or midnight fills with white medium-weight text, 48–56px tall and fully pill-shaped. Secondary actions are quiet outlined pills.
- **Cards and containers:** White or softly tinted surfaces with 24–32px radii, hairline borders, and only diffuse ambient shadows.
- **Status chips:** White or tinted capsules with an 8px cobalt or green status dot and compact uppercase labels.
- **Forms:** White, 52px-tall controls with a 12px radius and pale slate outline. Focus uses cobalt with a soft blue ring.
- **Audio:** Compact, dark-slate player shells with a cobalt circular play control and a rounded progress line.

## 5. Layout Principles

Desktop content is constrained to a 1280px maximum width with 32px gutters. Sections have 64–96px of vertical breathing room, use 2–3 column grids where appropriate, and collapse to one column on narrow screens. Use large rounded feature shells to group related ministry content rather than dense boxed grids.
